# Headhunters.lk Public Knowledge Base (Core Conversation Guide)

## 1. Candidate Questions
- **Are you currently hiring?** Yes. I will check the latest active vacancies in the Admin Knowledge Base before confirming what is available. Please tell me your preferred job title, industry, location and experience level so I can show relevant matches.
- **What vacancies are currently available?** Please tell me your preferred job title, industry, location and experience level. If no suitable vacancy is found, you can still submit your CV for future opportunities.
- **How can I apply for a job?** You can apply via our website, email your CV to info@headhunters.lk, or send it through WhatsApp to +94 77 397 5048. Please include the job title or reference number.
- **How can I submit my CV?** You can upload your CV through our website, email it to info@headhunters.lk, or send it via WhatsApp to +94 77 397 5048. Please include your full name, preferred position and current location.
- **Can I upload my CV through this chat?** Yes. Please use the attachment icon to upload your CV in PDF or DOCX format (max 10 MB). By submitting, you consent to Headhunters.lk storing and processing your information for recruitment purposes.
- **Where should I send it?** If it is your CV, you can attach it here, email it to info@headhunters.lk, or send it via WhatsApp to +94 77 397 5048.
- **Can I apply without a CV?** A CV is normally required. If your CV is not ready, you can provide your name, contact number, location, preferred position and work experience. Our team may ask you to submit your CV later.
- **What information should I provide with my CV?** Full name, Phone/WhatsApp number, Email address, Current location, Preferred job role, Relevant experience, Expected salary, Notice period, and Vacancy title/reference number.
- **Can I apply for more than one vacancy?** Yes. You may apply for multiple suitable vacancies. Clearly mention each position or job reference number.
- **I don’t know which job is suitable for me.** I can help identify suitable opportunities. Please provide your current job title, total experience, qualifications, key skills, preferred location and expected salary.
- **Do you have jobs for freshers?** Entry-level vacancies depend on current client requirements. Please share your qualifications, skills, preferred field and location.
- **Do you have overseas vacancies?** Available countries and overseas roles depend on the latest verified vacancy documents in the Admin Knowledge Base.
- **What happens after I submit my CV?** Our recruitment team will review your CV against current vacancies. If your profile matches a position, a consultant may contact you. Submission does not guarantee placement.
- **How long will it take to receive a response?** Response times depend on the vacancy and client selection process. Candidates whose profiles match will be contacted.
- **How can I check my application status?** Please provide your full name, phone number and the job title or reference number. I will record a follow-up request for the recruitment team.
- **Can I update my CV?** Yes. Upload or send your latest CV and mention that it is an updated version using the same email address or phone number.
- **Will my information remain confidential?** Yes, we process candidate information for recruitment purposes in accordance with our privacy policy.
- **Can you guarantee that I will get a job?** No recruitment agency can guarantee placement. Selection depends on your qualifications, experience, interview performance, and the employer’s final decision.
- **How should I prepare for an interview?** Review the job description and company background, prepare examples of your experience, dress professionally and join or arrive on time. Our consultants may provide additional guidance.

## 2. Client and Employer Questions
- **What recruitment services do you offer?** Executive search, Permanent recruitment, Mass recruitment, Confidential hiring, Talent acquisition, HR consulting, Workforce planning, Salary benchmarking, Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO).
- **How can Headhunters help my company?** We help employers identify, attract and recruit qualified professionals to reduce hiring risks and shorten recruitment cycles.
- **How can I submit a vacancy?** You can submit your vacancy requirements through www.headhunters.lk, email info@headhunters.lk, or call/WhatsApp +94 77 397 5048. I can also collect the initial requirement here (Job title, location, number of vacancies, required experience, expected salary range).
- **What information do you need from an employer?** Company name, Contact person and designation, Phone number and email, Job title, Number of vacancies, Job location, Responsibilities, Qualifications and experience, Salary range and benefits, Working days and hours, Required joining date.
- **What industries do you recruit for?** We support recruitment across multiple industries and professional levels.
- **Do you recruit senior executives?** Yes, we provide executive search and confidential hiring services for senior, managerial and specialized positions.
- **Can you handle bulk recruitment?** Yes, our mass-recruitment solutions can support organizations hiring multiple employees.
- **Can you handle confidential recruitment?** Yes, we provide confidential hiring services for sensitive replacements and senior appointments.
- **How much do your recruitment services cost?** The standard success-based recruitment fee is 75% of the selected candidate's one-month gross salary, subject to the signed client agreement.
- **Do I have to pay before you find a candidate?** Our standard recruitment service follows a "No Placement, No Fee" model.
- **Do you offer a replacement guarantee?** Standard terms include a 90-day replacement period if the placed candidate resigns or is terminated due to performance/conduct.
- **How long does recruitment take?** The timeline depends on the role, candidate availability, and interview process. Our consultant can provide a realistic timeline once we receive the brief.
- **Can you source candidates from all over Sri Lanka?** Yes.
- **Can you screen and shortlist candidates?** Yes, based on the agreed job requirements.
- **Can you arrange interviews?** Yes, our team can coordinate interviews and assist with communication.
- **Can you help us plan salaries?** Yes, we provide salary benchmarking and workforce-planning support.
